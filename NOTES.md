## pull. 2.1.0 — Boards, loops and covers

- Reference board: every frame you capture lands on today's board, a page with the frame, the source link at that exact moment, the site and the timecode. Open it from the popup, add notes, remove frames, and print or save it as a PDF to share.
- GIF / WebP: set IN and OUT (in the popup or on the player's timeline) and press GIF to get a looping clip of that part, up to 30 s. Pick GIF or WebP and the size in Settings.
- COVER: hover the thumbnail in the popup and press COVER to save the video's cover at the highest resolution the site has.
- Board, loop and cover files go in Boards, Loops and Thumbnails inside your pull. folder.

**Install:** Windows → pull-setup-win.exe · Mac → pull-setup-mac.zip, or the one-line installers in the README.

Already on 2.0.2 or newer: click Update in the pull. popup.
