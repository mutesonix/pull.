## pull. 2.1.1

- Reference board: a new All boards page lists every day's board, newest first, with its frames and clips. Get there from ← All boards (or the pull. logo) on any board.
- Boards from earlier days get the new button the next time you capture a frame.
- The popup's board row opens All boards when there are no captures yet today.

**Install:** Windows → pull-setup-win.exe · Mac → pull-setup-mac.zip, or the one-line installers in the README.

Already on 2.0.2 or newer: click Update in the pull. popup.
