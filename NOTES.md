## pull. 2.0.3 — Trim on the timeline

- Trim right on the video's timeline: press the new [ ] button in the player, then drag the IN and OUT brackets on the progress bar. The video scrubs as you drag, and I / O set a point at the playhead.
- PULL in the trim bar downloads just IN → OUT, and the popup's Range picks up the same points (and the brackets follow if you type them in the popup).
- YouTube: the pull. buttons now sit dead centre in their pill. YouTube's own icon sizing was pushing them low and to the right.
- Stills: the Generate button no longer spills out of the card.

**Install:** Windows → pull-setup-win.exe · Mac → pull-setup-mac.zip, or the one-line installers in the README.

Already on 2.0.2 or newer: click Update in the pull. popup.
